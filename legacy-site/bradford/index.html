About this candidate

Brad Bradford is the city councillor for Beaches–East York (elected 2018) and a former city planner. In the 2023 mayoral byelection he placed 8th, with approximately 1.5% of the vote (~9,000 ballots). He officially declared his 2026 mayoral candidacy on May 1, 2026, among the first wave of candidates to file. His campaign platform centres on what he calls "the three C's": crime, congestion, and cost of living.

This site documents his public political content. It does not assess his electoral viability and does not rank him among other candidates.

Positions

Stated stances on civic and policy topics. A position is anything the author has explicitly said they think, support, oppose, or observe about a governance issue. Verbatim source quote attached to every record.

Pledges

Future-tense commitments. "I will…", "If elected I will…", "We will keep pushing for…". Conditional pledges (contingent on election outcome) are marked.

Actions

Past-tense actions: motions brought to council, votes cast, projects supported, interventions made. The "Done" half of Said vs. Done — to be cross-referenced against the City of Toronto Council voting record in a later phase.

Endorsements

Named public endorsements — both received and given.

Appearances

Public appearances at named events: town halls, community gatherings, ribbon cuttings. Geographic and demographic coverage of campaign activity.
